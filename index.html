A grant DAO that reads its own proposals — and can't be talked into paying.

Anyone can fund the treasury. An applicant submits a proposal and posts a bond in the same call. An on-chain jury then fetches the proposal text and scores it against the funding standards: approved proposals can be paid from the treasury and get the bond back, rejected proposals have the bond slashed into the treasury. Every unit of GEN is accounted for in either direction.

How it works

  1. Anyone calls fund_treasury (payable) — the GEN becomes contract custody.
  2. An applicant calls submit_proposal (payable) — the sent GEN is the anti-spam bond.
  3. evaluate_proposal runs the jury: it fetches the proposal URL on-chain, rules against the funding standards and returns a 0–100 score.
  4. Once the jury has ruled, anyone can settle: fund_proposal pays an approved grant and returns its bond, reject_proposal slashes a rejected bond. An idle admin cannot stall either, and each bond settles exactly once.
  5. evaluate_proposal enforces aggregate solvency: an approval only becomes a payable liability when committed + requested ≤ treasury. Otherwise the proposal is UNFUNDED (jury approved, treasury cannot cover) and anyone can activate_proposal after a top-up.
  6. An UNFUNDED proposal's bond is never stranded: the applicant can call reclaim_unfunded_bond at any time — no treasury top-up needed. Reclaiming voids the grant, so nobody collects both.
  7. The admin can withdraw_treasury, but only the uncommitted balance — approved-but-unpaid grants are excluded, so a withdrawal can never leave an approved liability unpayable.
